# v0.3 romance

> thesis: the romance pass is legend swaps on the iso body, azerty for france and belgium, and qwerty-adjacent layouts for spain, portugal, italy with accent keys and dead keys. no new physical geometry beyond iso.

## evidenced facts

- azerty = qwerty with three swaps (a with q, z with w, m onto the semicolon key), the french and belgian standard `[evid-llog-020]`
- altgr third-level access on iso boards carries the accented characters `[evid-leg-007]`
- latin-script diacritics are a legend-set question, not geometry: the serbo-croatian/slovene line `[evid-llog-022]` is the same pattern (extra glyphs, same body) and it will hold for é è ç ñ as well

## open questions

- spanish (ñ), portuguese (ç), italian (accented vowels): per-country legend lines have no excerpts yet. flag for the v0.3 evidence pass.
- azerty's numbering row (french boards need shift for digits, an ergonomics complaint often raised): no corpus line, gather if it enters the pitch.
