# v1 cyrillic family

> thesis: the cyrillic family is one dual-legend pipeline with per-language glyph additions. russian jcuken is evidenced; every subtype adds glyphs the same way the latin passes do, and tajik rides the same pipeline market-parked.

## evidenced facts

- jcuken is the standard cyrillic layout across russian and eastern european keyboards `[evid-leg-012]`
- regional russian keyboards widely print dual legends: latin qwerty + cyrillic on the same caps `[evid-leg-011]`
- tajik: same pipeline plus six glyph forms (ғ ӣ қ ӯ ҳ ҷ), script-wise in at v1, market-wise parked (language ladder note)

## open questions

- ukrainian (ґ є і ї), belarusian, bulgarian (own layout lineage), serbian cyrillic (ђ ћ ј љ њ џ): per-language layout lines not yet excerpt-backed. gather per subtype when each turns on.
- kazakh: in transition toward latin script (official policy). if that lands, kazakh may migrate from v1.5 to v0.5. needs a source at pass time.
