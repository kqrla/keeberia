# v0.5 slavic + latin balkans

> thesis: the slavic latin pass adds glyphs, not geometry. the serbo-croatian latin and slovene boards carry five extra special characters over qwerty, and the polish and czech boards are the same pattern (diacritics on latin).

## evidenced facts

- serbo-croatian latin + slovene: five additional special characters (č ć ž š đ) over qwerty `[evid-llog-022]`
- the extra-glyphs-same-body pattern from v0.3 carries over: legend set change, no physical change
- serbia ships both latin and cyrillic faces of its keyboards, so a serbian buyer may want both legend sets in one order (cross-pass link to v1)

## open questions

- polish (ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż) and czech (háček/diakritika) specific legend lines: no excerpts yet, same gather pattern as v0.3.
- romanian and the latin balkan set: the corpus has the five-character line only for the serbo-croatian/slovene family. gather per-country before the pass.
