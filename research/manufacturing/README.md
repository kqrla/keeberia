# manufacturing

three concerns, kept separate on purpose:

- `processes/` — how boards and cases get made: fab processes, finishes, assembly, and the
  ways each process fails (defect modes). process knowledge explains *why* design rules exist
- `candidates/` — who can make it: fab and vendor research files
- `capabilities/` — a candidate's design rules as engine-consumable data

a candidate earns a capabilities.json only after its evidence lines exist.
